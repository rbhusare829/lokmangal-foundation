Whom does Lokmangal Foundation work for?

Lokmangal Foundation works for rural people. We work for the betterment of rural societies.

How can I help?

All types of help are welcome. You can get involved with Lokmangal Foundation with volunteering your time, donating money or in kinds, sponsoring a student or fundraising in your community.

Can I sponsor a child?

Yes, you can sponsor students at Lokmangal Foundation.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes, we are a Registered Charitable Trust. Your donations are deductible under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

How can I contact you?

Please see our Contact page for more details.

Are your projects applicable to the States other than Maharashtra?

Yes, we would like to work for rural societies in other States too.

Can foreign citizens donate online?

Yes, please visit Donate tab for more details.

What will my donation be used for?

Your donation will be used for one of our four projects where it will be needed at that time. If you want to donate for any particular project, you can tell us and we will use the donation for that project.

Why should I support Lokmangal Foundation?

Lokmangal Foundation works for the betterment of rural societies. We work to improve their standard of living by all means. If you want these people to advance with time, you can join us.

Why Lokmangal Foundation works only for rural societies?

Rural communities are the ones who suffer from financial deficiencies and have less access to advanced facilities. With our projects, Lokmangal Foundation is trying to take off their burdens arise due to lack of finances and bring them into the development stream.
